Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE distinctive app icon concept for SideBrowser, a companion browser that lives beside your main webpage. The earlier design was a generic three-rectangle sidebar layout; this new design needs an ownable, memorable silhouette through the metaphor of a companion page / bookmark. Visual: a single compact softly rounded graphite-gray main-page outline, nearly square, with an OPEN GAP along its right edge. A vivid Chrome-blue vertical companion page hooks through that open right edge and projects a little to the right, like a thick blue bookmark embracing the main page. The blue page has a single beautifully simple turned/folded lower corner formed by a small white triangular negative space, giving a tactile 'page' identity without 3D. Gray main page on left and blue companion on right visually interlock, a clear friendly relationship. Make the two forms feel like one carefully designed continuous emblem, not overlapping generic app windows. The blue side page is the hero, about one third of total width, with a slightly taller top edge than the gray outline to produce gentle asymmetry. Do not use the old long top toolbar bar, dots, tiny content lines, or large block-filled left panel. Be confident, minimal, bold and geometric; a single clever silhouette that can survive 16px display. Palette: neutral graphite #747B84 and vivid blue #4285F4, white negative space, no other chromatic colors. Flat perfectly uniform fills, precise optical spacing, smooth corners, no outlines thinner than roughly 7 percent of the emblem width. Compose on a white rounded-square app tile surrounded by a pale neutral gray #F1F3F4 opaque square canvas. Emblem is centered and occupies about 68 percent of canvas width, generous clean breathing room. ONE icon preview, front-on, opaque solid background throughout. No text or letters, no wordmark, no collage, no mockup, no 3D, no bevels, no gradients, no textures, no shadows, no watermark, no transparency extraction. Aim for iconic simplicity with one unmistakable blue folded side-page gesture.
